New features include:

- Reset button in top right with a prompt when clicked so there are no accidental clicks.

- When you click on the gold or 5th checkbox, the gun itself highlights gold. This same
  thing happens when you progress and the colors will differentiate your progress so you see 
  a visual difference.

- When all guns underneath a gun type are gold or anything after gold then the gun type will
  match the color as far as you have gotten on your camo grind.
